    How this differs from the Options Desk

    The Option-Selling Desk picks strikes and structures for selling premium against an IV/HV20 regime — it has no equivalent for a directional perpetual future. This desk instead turns NOCTUA's validated outputs (volatility-amplification probability and barrier touch curves) plus funding into stop-loss, take-profit, and a position-size multiplier for whichever side (long/short) you've already decided to take. It does not tell you which side to take.
    Method. SL/TP are picked from NOCTUA's barrier_curves (19h horizon) at the touch-probability targets above; falls back to a plain HV20 daily-move multiple if barrier curves aren't in the payload. Size multiplier shrinks when NOCTUA's p(vol amplify) is high, and again when funding is extreme in your direction. Horizon mismatch: barrier curves are calibrated to 19 hours — if you plan to hold much longer or shorter, treat these levels as an approximation, not a precise number. No liquidation modelling — always check your exchange's margin/liquidation price separately. Not financial advice.
